In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your profile, personal code, invites and memberships are erased, shared tasks you created are archived and emptied, and your sign-in is removed.

Without the app

Email support@imdone.me from the address on your account and we will delete it. Tasks stored only on your device are removed by uninstalling the app.
