1. What I'm Done does

I'm Done turns what you say into tasks, reminds you until they are done, and lets you share a task with people you invite. This policy applies to the app (iOS, Android, web) and the services behind it.

3. Who receives data (service providers)

Full, current list: subprocessors.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) — accounts, shared tasks, notifications. Data location: London (europe-west2).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers) — our small server that relays notifications and voice requests. It does not store audio or tasks.
  • Groq, Inc. (US) — speech-to-text (Whisper) and the language model that reads the task out of your words. Receives the audio and transcript for that request.
  • Google (Gemini API) — fallback speech/understanding provider, used only if the primary provider fails. Receives the audio/transcript for that request.
  • OpenAI — some Groq-hosted models (e.g. gpt-oss) are OpenAI open-weight models running on Groq's servers; no data is sent to OpenAI itself. If we ever call OpenAI directly this section and /subprocessors will be updated first.
  • Apple / Google — sign-in providers; and, only if you choose the on-device speech option, your phone's speech service (Apple Speech / Google speech recognition), under their policies.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) — hosts the web app and invitation links; sees ordinary web request data (IP address).
  • RevenueCat / Apple App Store / Google Play — only once paid plans are enabled.

Other people on a shared task can see your display name and your actions on that task.

4. International transfers

Firebase data is held in London. Voice requests are processed by providers in the United States (Groq, Google, Cloudflare's network). Transfers rely on your explicit consent (starting a voice recording, with an on-device alternative always available) and on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses / data-processing terms (GDPR Art. 46 / 49(1)(a); Saudi PDPL Art. 29 and Transfer Regulations; Jordanian PDPL). You may avoid all voice transfers by choosing Settings → Voice → on-device.

5. How long we keep data

6. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), the EU/UK GDPR and the Jordanian PDPL (No. 24 of 2023) you may: be informed; access your data (Settings → Account → *Download my data*); correct it; erase it (Settings → Account → *Delete my account*, or /delete-account); restrict or object to processing; port your data; withdraw consent at any time (sign out/delete, change Settings → Voice, or email us to stop marketing); and complain to the regulator — in Saudi Arabia the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA); in the EU your national supervisory authority; in the UK the ICO.

Email privacy@[YOUR-DOMAIN]. We reply within 30 days (and tell you if we need longer for a complex request). We may ask you to prove it is your account.

7. Children

You must be 16 or older to create an account; sign-up asks you to confirm this and does not proceed otherwise. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account we delete it. The app without an account (personal tasks on the device) collects nothing.

8. Security

Encrypted connections; access rules that let you read only tasks you belong to; no audio storage; push credentials held only on our server. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk we notify the authorities within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay (see our incident response plan).
